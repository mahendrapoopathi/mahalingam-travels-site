Frequently Asked Questions

Can we book for outstation trips?

Yes, we specialize in outstation trips across Tamil Nadu and neighboring states. We provide comfortable vehicles with experienced drivers for long-distance travel.

What's included in the price?

Our price includes vehicle rental, experienced driver, fuel charges, toll charges, and driver accommodation. Food and customer accommodation are not included.

How many hours per day are allowed?

For local trips: 8-10 hours per day. For outstation trips: 12-14 hours per day with adequate rest breaks for the driver.

Is night driving allowed?

Night driving is allowed for experienced drivers on familiar routes. However, we recommend starting early morning for safety and better sightseeing opportunities.

How do I make a booking?

You can book through our website by filling the booking form, or directly contact us via WhatsApp at +91 98657 82932. We'll confirm availability and provide fare details immediately.

Do you provide AC vehicles?

Yes, all our vehicles are well-maintained. We ensure comfortable travel in all weather conditions with clean, sanitized interiors.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 24 hours before travel are eligible for full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur charges. Please contact us for specific cases and emergency situations.
